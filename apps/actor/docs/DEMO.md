# Three-minute demo

1. **Problem.** African SME opportunities are scattered across government, foundation and programme websites.
2. **Input.** Run `examples/africa-sme.json`: women-owned fashion SMEs in Nigeria and Ghana.
3. **Run.** Show source selection, pages processed, duplicates removed, and dataset items saved.
4. **Dataset.** Point out different types (loan, accelerator, training, competition), countries, eligibility, deadline, status, and `sourceUrl`.
5. **Export.** JSON and CSV. Mention API, schedule, and webhook.
6. **AI (optional).** Repeat with `examples/africa-sme-ai.json` and a secret OpenAI key. Show `ai.summary`, match reasons, and that failed AI is not charged.
7. **Pricing.** Customers pay per valid opportunity. AI is optional and BYOK.
